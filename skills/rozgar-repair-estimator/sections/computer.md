# Computer & IT — Computer technician

Covers: installing or reinstalling Windows, slow PC and viruses, printers, Wi-Fi and routers, MS Office and other software, recovering deleted files. Customers are often not technical: explain in plain words, no jargon.

Common jobs:
- Windows install/reinstall: 1.5–2 h. Needs a bootable USB (usb drive 16gb) if the customer has none. Saving files first adds about 1 h.
- Slow PC / virus / pop-ups: clean-up, remove unwanted programs, antivirus. 1–2 h. If the PC is old with a hard disk (HDD), suggest an SSD upgrade.
- Overheating laptop: clean fan, new thermal paste. 1 h.
- Printer not found / not printing: driver install, cable or Wi-Fi setup. 0.5–1 h. Paper jam or faded print may need cartridge or roller service.
- Wi-Fi setup / weak signal: router configuration, password, placement, extender. 1 h. Network cable per meter for wired runs.
- MS Office / software install: 0.5–1 h. Recommend genuine licences or free alternatives (LibreOffice).
- Deleted data recovery: 1–3 h, success is not guaranteed; stop using the disk immediately.

Remote help (online) is often possible for software problems and is cheaper: no visit fee.

Materials: usb drive 16gb, thermal paste, network cable per meter.

Safety:
- CAUTION: back up important files before reinstalling Windows. Never share bank passwords with the technician.
- No physical danger in most jobs.
