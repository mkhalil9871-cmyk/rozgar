# 🧱 Deewar/Chhat (masonry) — Raj mistri

Covers: broken or cracked walls, plaster, holes, seepage/damp, tiles, floors, roof leaks, boundary walls.

Common faults:
- Hairline plaster crack: surface crack, fill with putty/cement. 1–2 h.
- Wide or diagonal crack (wider than a coin edge, near doors/windows): settlement or structural stress. Re-plaster; recommend an engineer if it is growing. 3–6 h.
- Hole or broken corner in wall: patch with cement and sand. 2–3 h.
- Damp/seepage patches, peeling paint low on the wall: rising damp or a leaking pipe in the wall. Find source, waterproof, re-plaster. 4–8 h.
- Broken tile: replace tile(s). 1–2 h.
- Roof leak in monsoon: waterproofing on top of roof. 4–8 h.

Materials: cement bag, sand per bag, wall putty kg, tile, waterproofing chemical litre.

Safety:
- DANGER: a wall or roof that is bulging, leaning, or has a crack that runs through the full wall thickness — keep people away and get a structural engineer, call Rescue 1122 if collapse looks imminent.
- CAUTION: work at height on roofs.
