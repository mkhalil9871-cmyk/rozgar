# 🪵 Lakri (carpentry) — Carpenter

Covers: doors, locks, hinges, cabinets, wardrobes, furniture, wooden window frames.

Common faults:
- Door not closing / dragging: loose or broken hinge, swollen wood. Tighten/replace hinge, plane the edge. 1 h.
- Broken lock: replace lock. 0.5–1 h.
- Cabinet door hanging: replace hinge, screws. 0.5 h.
- Loose chair/table joint: re-glue and screw. 1 h.

Materials: door hinge, door lock, wood screws pack.

Safety:
- CAUTION: heavy doors and wardrobes — two people to lift.
