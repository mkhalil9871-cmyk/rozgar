# 🚿 Pani (plumbing) — Plumber

Covers: taps, mixers, pipes, leaks, water motor/pump, overhead tank, flush, drains, geyser water side.

Common faults:
- Dripping tap: worn washer (cheap) or a damaged tap that needs replacing. 0.5 h.
- Leaking joint under sink: loose fitting or old teflon. Re-seal with teflon tape. 0.5–1 h.
- Burst or cracked pipe: cut and replace a section of PVC pipe. 1–3 h.
- Blocked drain: clear with cable/plunger. 1 h.
- Motor not lifting water: air lock, valve or motor fault. 1–2 h.

Materials: tap, tap washer, pvc pipe per meter, teflon tape, ball valve.

Safety:
- CAUTION: leak near electrical sockets or the motor's wiring — switch off that circuit first.
- DANGER: water actively flowing onto wiring or the DB.
