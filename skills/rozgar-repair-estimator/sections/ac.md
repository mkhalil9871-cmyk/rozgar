# ❄️ AC/Fridge — AC technician

Covers: split/window AC, inverter AC, fridge and freezer.

Common faults:
- AC not cooling: dirty filters/coil (service), low gas, or bad capacitor. Service 1–2 h; gas refill only if a leak is found and fixed.
- AC outdoor unit not starting: capacitor failure. 0.5–1 h.
- Water dripping from indoor unit: blocked drain pipe. 0.5 h.
- Fridge not cooling: thermostat, gas or compressor relay. 1–2 h.

Materials: ac gas refill, ac capacitor.

Safety:
- CAUTION: AC capacitors hold charge; power off before opening.
- Watch for overcharging: a gas refill without finding the leak is a common upsell.
