# ⚡ Bijli (electrical) — Electrician

Covers: switches, sockets, wiring, distribution board (DB), breakers, fans, lights, UPS/inverter, solar wiring, earthing.

Common faults:
- Burnt or melted switch/socket (black marks): loose connection or overload. Replace switch/socket, tighten wires. 0.5–1 h.
- Breaker keeps tripping: overload or short circuit. Find faulty appliance/circuit, may need new breaker. 1–2 h.
- Fan slow or humming: bad capacitor. Replace capacitor. 0.5 h.
- Light flickering: loose connection or failing holder/driver. 0.5 h.
- UPS not charging / battery issue: check wiring and battery terminals. 1 h.

Materials: switch, socket, circuit breaker, wire per meter, ceiling fan capacitor.

Safety:
- DANGER: sparking, burning smell, smoke, hot or melted socket, water near wiring or DB, exposed live wires. Tell the user to switch off the main breaker and call FESCO 118 if the fault is at the meter.
- CAUTION: any work inside the DB. Main must be off before work.
