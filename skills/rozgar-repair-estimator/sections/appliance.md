# Home appliances — Appliance technician

Covers: washing machines, fridges and freezers, water pumps/motors, microwaves and ovens, irons, blenders and other small appliances.

These are closed machines: the customer often cannot photograph the fault. Diagnose from the symptoms they tick and from a photo of the model sticker if they give one. Name the most likely cause first and say what the technician should check.

Common faults:
- Washing machine does not spin: worn belt, faulty timer or motor capacitor. 1–2 h.
- Washing machine water does not drain: blocked filter/pipe or faulty drain pump. 1 h.
- Washing machine noisy/shaking: uneven load, worn bearings or loose drum. 1–3 h.
- Fridge not cooling: thermostat, gas leak or compressor relay. 1–2 h.
- Fridge too much ice: door seal or defrost timer. 1 h.
- Water pump hums but no water: stuck impeller or motor capacitor; or an air lock. 1 h.
- Motor gets very hot: dry running or bearing failure. 1–2 h.
- Microwave does not heat: magnetron or fuse. 1 h.
- Small appliance does not turn on: fuse, cord or switch. 0.5 h.

Materials: washing machine timer, drain pump, motor capacitor, fridge thermostat, fuse.

Safety:
- DANGER: sparks inside a microwave, burning smell, or water near the plug. Unplug at once.
- CAUTION: unplug every appliance before opening it; fridges and motors have capacitors that hold charge.
