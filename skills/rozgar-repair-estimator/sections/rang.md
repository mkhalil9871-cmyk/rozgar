# 🎨 Rang (painting) — Painter

Covers: repainting rooms or walls, damp stains, peeling paint, polish on wood.

Common faults:
- Peeling/flaking paint: sand, prime, repaint. About 1 litre emulsion per 10 m² per coat.
- Damp stain: fix the damp first (deewar section), then primer and paint.
- Faded wall: two coats of emulsion.

Labour: roughly 1 hour per 8–10 m² per coat.

Materials: emulsion paint litre, primer litre, sandpaper sheet.

Safety:
- CAUTION: ventilate the room; keep children away from fresh paint.
- If peeling is caused by seepage, say so: painting alone will not last.
