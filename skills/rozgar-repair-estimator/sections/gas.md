# 🔥 Gas — Gas fitter

Covers: gas pipes, valves, stoves, heaters, geyser gas side.

Common faults:
- Smell of gas: leaking joint or valve. ALWAYS DANGER.
- Stove burner weak flame: blocked jet, clean it. 0.5 h.
- Heater not lighting: thermocouple/valve. 1 h.
- New heater or stove fitting: pipe and valve. 1–2 h.

Materials: gas pipe per meter, gas valve.

Safety:
- DANGER for any gas smell or leak: close the main gas valve, open windows, do not switch any lights or appliances on or off, no flames. Call SNGPL 1199 immediately. Do not post the job until the area is safe.
- Unvented heaters in closed rooms in winter can kill by carbon monoxide — warn the user.
